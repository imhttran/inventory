# Demo Video — Narration Track

Ready-to-paste narration for a TTS engine (ElevenLabs, Play.ht, macOS
"say -v Samantha" style voices excluded — this is written for a **warm,
American male, conversational** read).

Suggested TTS settings (ElevenLabs): a natural/conversational male voice,
Stability ~0.5, Similarity ~0.75, Style ~0.2, Speed 0.95–1.0. Generate one
block per scene so you can align each clip to its screen recording.

Pacing guide: ~140 words per minute. Total read ≈ 3 minutes. The `[beat]`
markers are natural pauses — most TTS engines respect "…" or a line break;
insert a small silence in your editor where marked.

---

## Cold open (over the login screen) — ~10s

> Every part, every bin, every sale — accounted for. This is HTT Tiers,
> a full auto-parts inventory platform. Let me show you around.

## Scene 1 — Admin creates a user — ~10s

> From the dashboard, I can add a new user in seconds — just an email and a
> temporary password. … And there they are, right in the users list.
>
> (The login and 2FA moment plays silently — let the visuals carry it.)

## Scene 2 — The new user's first login — ~30s

> Now Danny signs in for the first time.
>
> The app walks him through everything himself — a new password… his
> profile… and then a personalized dashboard.
>
> Right away, he can search the parts catalog — live results as he types.

## Scene 3 — What a client can do — ~30s

> Here's the same app through a customer's eyes.
>
> They can browse the full catalog, build a cart, adjust quantities —
> everything reads instantly.
>
> But the charge button stays disabled. Checkout is a staff action — and the
> backend enforces that, not just the interface.

## Scene 4 — Staff in action — ~45s

> Now the counter.
>
> Staff signs in, and the sale goes through in one click. Every line posts a
> signed row to the ledger, and stock updates atomically.
>
> Over in inventory, everything is live — the sale we just rang up is already
> in the movement history.
>
> This alternator is below its reorder point. A delivery just arrived, so
> I'll receive ten units… and it's healthy again.
>
> And when parts move between bins, the transfer writes both sides of the
> move — out of one bin, into the other. Every change is accounted for.

## Scene 5 — Admin controls close it out — ~25s

> And administration ties it all together — roles, verification, password
> resets, all managed from one screen.
>
> One app. Three levels of access. And a complete audit trail behind every
> change.

## Optional inserts

**Suppliers (staff)** — ~15s:

> Purchasing lives here too — vendors with contacts, codes, and addresses.
> Adding a supplier takes one form.

**Add Product (staff)** — ~20s:

> New parts are just as quick — SKU, part number, price, brand, and category.
> Even the brand list grows on the fly.

**Search page** — ~15s:

> And when you need more than a quick look, there's a dedicated full-text
> search — by name, SKU, part number, brand, or category.
